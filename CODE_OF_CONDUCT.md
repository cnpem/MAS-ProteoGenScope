# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to maintaining a professional, inclusive and respectful environment for scientific and software collaboration around ProteoGenScope.

## Expected behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and constructively;
- focus criticism on ideas, methods and code rather than individuals;
- document analytical assumptions and limitations clearly;
- protect confidential, clinical and personally identifiable information;
- avoid committing credentials, private data or restricted infrastructure details.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory behavior, deliberate disclosure of confidential information, and abusive or disruptive communication are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Concerns should be raised through the project maintainers or the appropriate institutional channels at CNPEM.
